# Risk Checks

Apply risk checks before final recommendations. Do not call a journal safe unless relevant checks were actually completed.

## Hard Exclusions

Remove a journal from final recommendations when verified evidence shows:

| Condition | Reason |
|---|---|
| Listed as predatory in a credible standalone journal list | High publication-risk |
| Publisher or journal appears on a credible predatory list and no reliable contrary evidence exists | High publication-risk |
| CAS warning list high-risk status | Often unacceptable for Chinese academic evaluation |
| Official scope clearly mismatches the manuscript | Bad fit regardless of metrics |
| User explicitly rejects the publisher, OA model, APC range, or indexing status | Violates constraints |

List excluded journals separately with the reason and source.

## Soft Warnings

Keep the journal only with a visible warning when relevant:

| Condition | Warning |
|---|---|
| ESCI only, not SCI/SSCI | Some graduation rules may not accept it |
| Self-citation rate above 20% | IF reliability risk |
| Very high annual article volume | Possible quality-control or reputation concern |
| CAS warning list low-risk status | Confirm with supervisor |
| APC unknown or high | Budget risk |
| Review time uncertain or very long | Deadline risk |
| Indexing data conflicts across sources | Verify manually before submission |

## Recommended Checks

Use sources appropriate to the field:

- CAS warning list or LetPub warning-list fields for Chinese academic context.
- Beall-style lists only as one signal; disclose source limitations.
- DOAJ for open-access legitimacy.
- COPE membership for publisher practice signal.
- Web of Science, Scopus, PubMed, or discipline-specific indexing where relevant.
- Official publisher page for journal ownership, APC, and policies.

## Risk Language

Use precise language:

- Good: "未能完成 Beall-style 名单核查，建议投稿前手动确认。"
- Good: "该刊显示为 ESCI，若学校要求 SCI/SSCI，请先确认是否认可。"
- Bad: "该刊绝对安全。"
- Bad: "不在 Beall's List，所以没有风险。"
